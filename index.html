Who We Are

More Than Real Estate.
A Life Advisory.

Boca Prime is a private real estate and life advisory firm based in Boca Raton. We serve individuals, families, and investors who want more than a transaction — they want a trusted guide through every step of building their Florida life.

Whether you're buying a home, starting a business, investing capital, or relocating your family — we connect you with the right properties, attorneys, accountants, and community.

  • Residential homes, condos & waterfront properties
  • Business acquisition and franchise advisory
  • Investment strategy with real numbers, not hype
  • Trusted referrals to attorneys, CPAs & lenders
  • Full relocation support for families moving to Florida
  • Bilingual — English & Turkish spoken
