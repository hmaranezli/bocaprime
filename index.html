Who We Are

Boutique Advisory.
Exceptional Results.

Boca Prime is a boutique real estate advisory firm based in Boca Raton, Florida. We serve buyers, sellers, and investors who expect more than a transaction — they expect a true partnership.

With deep roots in South Florida and a multilingual team, we specialize in guiding international clients — including Turkish investors — through every step of the U.S. real estate process with transparency and expertise.

  • Exclusive access to off-market properties across South Florida
  • Multilingual advisory — English & Turkish spoken
  • Full transaction coordination, legal & financial guidance
  • Tailored investment strategy for international buyers
  • Trusted relocation support for families and executives
